Kai privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Who uses Kai

Kai is a personal assistant used only by its developer, Sadeq Albaharna, with his own Google account. It has no other users.

What Kai reads

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): bank and payment notices, to keep a record of his own balances and spending.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): his events, to tell him what his day and week hold.

Kai never sends, changes or deletes email or calendar events.

Where the data goes

What Kai reads is kept on a server he owns and is used only to answer him. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the stored copies are deleted from his server on request.
